Rehab / movement preparation

Use this as low-load movement practice, not a diagnosis-specific treatment plan. Ideally have a sports PT assess hip, knee, trunk, shoulder and elbow symptoms and tailor the exercises.

  • 90/90 breathing with feet supported: 2 sets of 4–5 slow breaths; relaxed neck and jaw, no forced bracing.
  • Side-lying hip abduction: 2×10 each side, slow and controlled; stop if it reproduces lateral knee pain.
  • Supported single-leg balance: 2×20–30 sec each side; pelvis level, knee tracking comfortably over foot.
  • Low step-down or sit-to-stand: 2×6 each side only if pain-free and controlled; use a small range.
  • Bird dog: 2×6 each side with 3-sec holds; keep trunk quiet, do not rotate.
  • Shoulder external-rotation isometric: 3×20 sec at easy effort, elbow supported; only if shoulder/elbow tolerate it.
  • Scapular wall slide: 2×8 in a comfortable range; no shrugging or painful range.

Frequency: 10–15 minutes, 4–6 days/week, easy effort. Quality over fatigue.

Symptom rules: Keep exercise discomfort at 0–2/10 and back to baseline by the next morning. Stop or regress if pain rises, changes your mechanics, causes limping, or persists/worsens afterward. New weakness, numbness, significant swelling, locking/giving-way, or worsening shoulder/elbow weakness warrants prompt clinical assessment. Do not train through progressive weakness.
